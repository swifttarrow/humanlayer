You are a senior product + AI systems engineer.

Your task is to transform a rough set of requirements into a **lightweight, research-oriented PRD**.

IMPORTANT:
- Do NOT over-specify implementation details
- Do NOT lock in technologies unless obvious
- Focus on identifying unknowns, tradeoffs, and research areas
- This document is for THINKING, not building

---

## Output Format

Produce a concise PRD (1–3 pages max) with the following sections:

### 1. Problem Framing
- What problem are we solving?
- Who is the user?
- What does success look like?

### 2. Core Use Cases
- 3–5 primary user flows
- Keep them high-level (no UI detail)

### 3. System Overview (Abstract)
- Key components (e.g., agent, server, UI, data layer)
- How they interact (no deep architecture)

### 4. Key Unknowns / Research Areas
Break into categories:
- Technical unknowns
- Product/design unknowns
- Data dependencies
- Scaling concerns

### 5. Critical Decisions
- List decisions that must be made before building
- For each: include 2–3 options and tradeoffs

### 6. Risks
- What could go wrong?
- Where are the hardest parts?

### 7. Research Plan
- What should be researched next?
- Suggested order of investigation
- What "good" answers would look like

---

## Style Guidelines
- Be concise and structured
- Prefer bullets over paragraphs
- Call out uncertainty explicitly
- Avoid fake precision
